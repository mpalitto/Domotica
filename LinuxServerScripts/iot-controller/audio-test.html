Audio test

Plays the same sounds the wall panel uses. Use it to tell apart “the page is not producing sound” from “the device is muted / volume is at zero”. 

Ready. Tap a button.

How to read the result:
• Media element works (play() resolved) but you hear nothing → the iPad is muted or its media volume is at zero. Open Settings → Sounds or drag the volume slider in Control Center (it appears while the loop tone plays) — the hardware buttons are not needed.
• play() rejected → the page is being blocked (autoplay policy / low-power mode).
• Nothing plays and no error → the tap did not reach the page (cached bundle, or the panel is showing a cached page).
